Sources and method

Original universe and profile snapshot: 13 November 2026. Watchlist mapping: 14 November 2026. Nifty 500 union the top 500 NSE companies by total share volume divided by all 64 trading sessions, 13 August to 13 November 2026. Renamed tickers merged; ETFs and DUMMYHEG excluded. Company source links remain on every entry. Comparison candidates use inherited industry tags, not a fresh verification of every company. NSE's BSE identity and two duplicate merges were separately verified.

Counts are companies, not money invested. Price check table: Yahoo Finance daily prices, 13 November 2026 close; split/bonus-adjusted history, not adjusted for dividends, rights issues or demergers; oldest-match and 52-week figures are as reliable as the available history, and rows with limited coverage are marked.
